Founder's Desk
"Maharashtra has never lacked enterprise. What our youth needed was not another lecture, but a dependable bridge from aspiration to bankable execution."

When I traveled through rural talukas and industrial clusters across Maharashtra, I saw countless young minds with immense grit but no access to capital, mentorship, or market linkages. They were told business was only for the privileged. We founded Mi Udyojak Honarach to shatter that myth permanently.

Our mission is to build a vibrant ecosystem where any first-generation dreamer—from Kolhapur to Nagpur—can transform traditional craftsmanship, agriculture, or technical skill into a resilient enterprise that creates jobs for generations to come.
